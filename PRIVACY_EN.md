# StructRule Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 25, 2026

StructRule respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

## Personal information

StructRule does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location. It does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics, or user tracking.

## File and folder access

StructRule accesses only files and folders that you explicitly select. This access is used to check folder structures and naming rules and to provide project creation and rule application features.

Operating-system access information, templates, rules, project information, and diagnostic logs may be stored locally on your device. This information is not automatically transmitted to external servers.

## Notifications

StructRule may use system notifications to report rule violations. Notifications are generated locally and do not pass through an external server. You can change notification permissions in your operating system settings.

## Third parties and external transmission

StructRule does not sell or provide your information or file contents to third parties. The app does not operate a server that receives personal information or usage data.

## Data management

You can remove registered projects in the app and delete related files directly. Local settings and stored data are kept in the operating system's application data area. Some settings may remain after uninstalling the app according to operating-system behavior.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app features or legal requirements change. Updates and their effective date will be posted on this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, use [StructRule Support Issues](https://github.com/yohns-dev/StructRule-Support/issues). Do not include personal information or sensitive file contents in a public issue.
